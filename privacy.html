لا تطلب المنصة ولا تخزّن العبارات السرية (Passphrase) أو المفاتيح الخاصة (Secret Seed) لمحافظ المستخدمين أو التطبيقات. تتم عمليات المصادقة والدفع عبر القنوات الرسمية المعتمدة من شبكة Pi.

  • تشغيل المنصة وتنفيذ عمليات العرض والطلب والمدفوعات المرتبطة بالخدمة.

Privacy Policy — MONEEB GLOBAL Sudan Agro

Last Updated: November 2026 | Platform: MONEEB GLOBAL Sudan Agro

1. Overview

MONEEB GLOBAL Sudan Agro respects user privacy and is committed to protecting information processed while using our agricultural B2B marketplace on Pi Browser and the Pi Network ecosystem.

2. Information We Process

  • Pi SDK Authentication Data: Public user identifiers/usernames permitted through Pi SDK authentication to manage sessions and orders within the app.
  • Marketplace & Transaction Records: Agricultural product listings, order details, and Pi payment status records required to fulfill transactions.
  • Technical & Operational Logs: Basic request and diagnostic logs used to maintain reliability, security, and performance.

3. Wallets & Secret Keys

We never request, collect, or store user passphrases or wallet secret seeds. Authentication and Pi transactions rely on official Pi Network SDK and ecosystem flows.

4. Use & Sharing of Information

  • Operate the marketplace, process orders, and verify Pi payment flows.
  • Maintain bilingual functionality (Arabic and English) and troubleshoot technical issues.
  • We do not sell user data to third-party data brokers, and only disclose data when required to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or inquiries regarding MONEEB GLOBAL Sudan Agro, please reach out through our official platform channels.
